Private & Institutional Lending

We facilitate secured and unsecured private lending solutions for businesses with diverse and complex capital requirements through private lenders, venture capital networks, strategic investors, and HNI & UHNI channels.

  • Institutional and professional lending
  • Private lender-led funding structures
  • Customised deal structuring after eligibility assessment
  • Published lending ticket size: INR 10 Lakhs to INR 1,000 Crores, subject to eligibility and due diligence

Final funding depends on borrower profile, legal and financial documentation, credit history, repayment track record and overall risk assessment.

Genuine Solutions for Poor CIBIL Profiles

We consider cases involving low or poor CIBIL scores, EMI bounces, late payments, settled or closed accounts and past credit issues only when supported by a genuine and valid reason.

  • Medical or family emergencies
  • COVID-related income impact
  • Business loss followed by income stability
  • Temporary financial setbacks that are now resolved
  • Job change, salary delay or temporary business slowdown

Each case is reviewed for current income stability, bank-statement cash flow, available security and future repayment capacity. We do not accept fraud, fake documents or intentional defaults. Approval is never guaranteed.

EMI Restructuring & EMI Stress Reduction

We assist clients facing high monthly EMI pressure, multiple loan commitments, reduced income or genuine financial hardship in exploring suitable restructuring options, strictly subject to lender policies.

  • Assessment of existing EMIs and income
  • Guidance on lender-approved restructuring options
  • Possible tenure adjustment to reduce monthly burden, subject to approval
  • Clear communication and responsible financial guidance

Cases require a genuine reason. We do not make false promises or guarantee EMI reductions.

Transparent Property-Based Loan Solutions

Property valuation for loan purposes is based on current market value and independent authorised valuer reports. Future or expected appreciation is not counted for initial loan approval, and artificial or inflated valuation is neither supported nor encouraged.

  • Valuation based on current market value
  • Loan-to-Value (LTV) norms as per bank/NBFC policy
  • Guidance on loan against property, top-up and balance-transfer possibilities
  • Transparent coordination with lenders

All valuations, loan amounts and approvals remain subject to the respective lender's policies and final decision.

Requirement-to-Disbursement Workflow

  1. Requirement understanding & client profiling: Understand funding needs, objectives, borrower profile and risk appetite.
  2. Credit, financial & risk assessment: Review credit history, income, banking conduct, liabilities and documentation.
  3. Internal expert review: Validate policy-aligned structuring, lender suitability and risk mitigation.
  4. Strategic bank & NBFC mapping: Match the profile with lender-specific credit policies and suitable options.
  5. Application filing & execution: Coordinate documentation, submission, lender communication and follow-ups.
  6. Sanction & disbursement support: Assist with sanction clarifications, conditions and disbursement coordination.
